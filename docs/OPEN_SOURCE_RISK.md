## 2. Private / biometric data

| item | count | verdict | why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data/processed/face_features/User_Me/*.npy` | 5 | **PRIVATE** | face tensors derived from the operator — biometric template data |
| `data/processed/voice_features/User_Me/*.npy` | 10 | **PRIVATE** | MFCC templates of the operator |
| `data/raw/face/User_Me/*` | 5 | **PRIVATE** | source media of the above |
| `data/raw/voice/User_Me/*` | 10 | **PRIVATE** | source media of the above |

Even though the features are not raw images, MFCC and 112×112 face tensors are
re-identifiable and count as biometric data in many jurisdictions. Do not ship
them — not in git, not in a release asset, not inside a container image.

**OPEN_SOURCE_SUBSTITUTE** (recommended shape)

```
data/            ->  .gitkeep only + docs/DATASET.md describing how to obtain data
data/samples/    ->  1 synthetic face tensor + 1 synthetic MFCC tensor (self-made)
tools/make_dummy_identity.py
                 ->  regenerates a folder that plays the role of User_Me from
                     (a) a chosen Olivetti subject for faces and
                     (b) a deterministic tone/noise wav for voice,
                     so the whole pipeline runs without any personal media
```
